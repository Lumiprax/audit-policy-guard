# Marketplace Account Actions

These items require the owner’s real business/account/legal information and are intentionally not fabricated by the Factory.

## Partner identity

1. Sign in to Atlassian Marketplace with the account that owns `SAGE Money Factory`.
2. Create a Marketplace Partner profile (formerly vendor profile).
3. Use the real individual/business entity that will own and receive revenue from the app.
4. Add an active private-domain business email. Paid-via-Atlassian submissions cannot use Gmail/Yahoo-style public domains.
5. Ensure at least one Partner contact is registered with the Atlassian Developer Community.

## Developer Space and distribution

1. Confirm the Developer Space is associated with the same Marketplace Partner.
2. Publish the Developer Space when Atlassian prompts during listing setup.
3. Enable Forge app sharing/distribution and link the production Forge app to the Marketplace listing.

## Commercial account setup

1. Select Paid via Atlassian.
2. Enter the approved Standard all-user pricing derived from `config/commercial-launch.json` and `config/pricing-strategy.json`.
3. Complete payout, tax, and required business verification information using the real receiving entity.

## Legal and public support

Current Lumiprax public identity now available:

- Website: `https://lumiprax.com`
- Support: `support@lumiprax.com`
- Public support page: `https://lumiprax.com/support`
- Privacy: `privacy@lumiprax.com` / `https://lumiprax.com/privacy`
- Security: `security@lumiprax.com` / `https://lumiprax.com/security`

1. Publish the finalized privacy notice on a public URL.
2. Publish a public support page and organization-domain support contact.
3. In the listing, choose Atlassian’s standard customizable end-user agreement if approved for this business.
4. Have appropriate legal counsel determine whether a DPA/provider-specific terms are required for the app’s transient processing of Jira audit data.
5. Accept the Marketplace Partner Agreement only after reviewing it and confirming authority to bind the owner entity.

## Listing assets

Capture the three required real in-product highlights and brand assets described in `docs/LISTING_ASSETS.md`. Do not use screenshots containing unrelated customer information, secrets, tokens, or personal account details.

The Factory should mark an item complete only after it has concrete evidence from the Marketplace/Developer Console or the corresponding public URL.
